Your career file. Two jobs: it is the **only** source of accomplishments a tailored resume may draw
from, and it holds the **answer bank** that fills application forms.

`/job` never invents a fact that is not in this file. A thin file produces thin resumes and blocked
applications, so the time spent here pays back on every run.
